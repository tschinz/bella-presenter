# Iosevka Nerd Font Mono

The bundled `IosevkaNerdFontMono-Regular.ttf` and `IosevkaNerdFontMono-Bold.ttf` are unmodified files from the
Nerd Fonts release v3.5.1 (<https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/releases/tag/v3.5.1>, archive `Iosevka.tar.xz`).

They are Iosevka 34.8.0 by Renzhi Li (<https://github.com/be5invis/Iosevka>), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (see `Iosevka-LICENSE.md`), patched by the Nerd Fonts project with the icon sets below. The patched
